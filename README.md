# Night Hum

Giraffes hum at night. Nobody knows why.

**Play it:** https://juleme.github.io/night-hum/

Hum into your mic. Your giraffe's neck fills with light, and the calves in the dark walk home to it. Each calf answers to one note: bring your light into its ring and hold it there. At dawn, hum the giraffe's own note.

- Works in Chrome and Safari, on a laptop or a phone. Headphones are optional.
- No mic, or somewhere you can't hum: choose "Play without a mic" and slide a finger up the neck.
- Your voice stays on your device. The page records nothing and sends nothing.

## The fact behind it

Researchers left microphones running in three European zoos for 947 hours and found a low hum, around 92 Hz, only at night. What it is for is not known. The hums you hear at sunrise in the game are two of those recordings.
